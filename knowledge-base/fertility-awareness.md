# Fertility awareness and the fertile window
source: https://www.acog.org/womens-health/infographics/fertility-awareness-based-methods-of-family-planning

The fertile window is the time when pregnancy is most likely if sperm and an egg are present. Calendar estimates are not exact because ovulation can vary from cycle to cycle. Tracking cycle dates, cervical mucus, and basal body temperature may provide information, but these methods require consistent use and do not guarantee pregnancy prevention.

Anyone seeking contraception or advice about trying to become pregnant should speak with a qualified healthcare professional.
