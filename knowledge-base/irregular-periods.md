# Irregular periods
source: https://womenshealth.gov/menstrual-cycle/period-problems

Periods may be irregular when the interval between periods changes, bleeding lasts longer than usual, or bleeding is unusually heavy. Stress, changes in weight or activity, medications, thyroid conditions, and polycystic ovary syndrome are among the possible reasons, but a healthcare professional must evaluate the individual situation.

Track the first day of bleeding, the number of days between periods, bleeding amount, and related symptoms. Seek medical care for very heavy bleeding, severe pain, fainting, pregnancy concerns, or persistent changes.
