# Sleep and the menstrual cycle
source: https://womenshealth.gov/menstrual-cycle/your-menstrual-cycle

Hormonal changes across the menstrual cycle can coincide with changes in sleep, energy, or mood. A consistent sleep schedule, a cool and quiet bedroom, regular daytime activity, and a relaxing wind-down routine may support sleep.

Persistent insomnia, loud snoring, breathing pauses, or sleep problems that affect daytime functioning should be discussed with a healthcare professional.
