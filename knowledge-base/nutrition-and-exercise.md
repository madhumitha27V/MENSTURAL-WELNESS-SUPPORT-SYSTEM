# Nutrition and physical activity
source: https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/healthy-diet

A balanced eating pattern includes a variety of foods such as vegetables, fruit, legumes, nuts, whole grains, and appropriate sources of protein. Individual nutrition needs vary, and restrictive diets or supplements should be discussed with a qualified professional.

Regular physical activity can support general health and wellbeing. Choose activities that are comfortable and appropriate for your health and energy level. A consistent sleep routine, a comfortable sleep environment, and relaxing wind-down activities may support better sleep during a cycle. Stop and seek medical advice if activity causes severe pain, fainting, chest pain, or unusual shortness of breath.
