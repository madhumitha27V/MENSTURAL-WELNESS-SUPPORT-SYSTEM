# Period pain and cramps
source: https://www.acog.org/womens-health/faqs/dysmenorrhea-painful-periods

Period cramps can sometimes be eased with a heating pad or hot water bottle, warm baths, gentle exercise, and rest. Some people use over-the-counter pain relievers, but a healthcare professional or pharmacist can help determine what is safe for an individual.

Pain that is severe, worsening, lasts beyond the first few days, or interferes with daily activities should be discussed with a healthcare professional. This information does not diagnose the cause of pain.
