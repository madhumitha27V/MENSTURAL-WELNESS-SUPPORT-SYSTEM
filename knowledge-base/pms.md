# Premenstrual syndrome (PMS)
source: https://www.acog.org/womens-health/faqs/premenstrual-syndrome-pms

Premenstrual syndrome can include physical and emotional symptoms that happen before a period. Symptoms can include bloating, breast tenderness, headaches, fatigue, mood changes, irritability, and cramps. Keeping a symptom diary over several cycles may help identify whether symptoms follow a pattern.

Regular sleep, physical activity, balanced meals, and reducing substances that worsen symptoms may help some people. Gentle heat, hydration, and light stretching may help some people with mild period cramps. Persistent, severe, or disruptive symptoms should be discussed with a healthcare professional.
