# Polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS)
source: https://www.womenshealth.gov/a-z-topics/polycystic-ovary-syndrome

Polycystic ovary syndrome is a common hormonal condition. Possible features include irregular menstrual periods, higher androgen levels, and polycystic ovaries, but experiences vary. A healthcare professional uses a medical history, examination, and appropriate tests to evaluate possible causes of irregular cycles.

A symptom or cycle tracker cannot diagnose PCOS. Seek professional care for persistent irregular periods, new or concerning symptoms, or questions about fertility or treatment.
