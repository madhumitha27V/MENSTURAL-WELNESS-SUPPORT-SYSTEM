# When to seek medical care
source: https://www.acog.org/womens-health/faqs/abnormal-uterine-bleeding

Contact a healthcare professional about bleeding that is unusually heavy or prolonged, bleeding between periods, bleeding after sex, or cycle changes that persist. Severe or worsening pelvic pain should also be evaluated.

Seek urgent medical care for heavy bleeding with weakness, fainting, shortness of breath, severe pain, or other emergency symptoms. If pregnancy is possible and there is pain or bleeding, contact a healthcare professional promptly. This information does not replace professional medical advice.
