# Menstrual cycle basics
source: https://www.acog.org/womens-health/faqs/your-menstrual-cycle

The menstrual cycle is counted from the first day of bleeding to the first day of the next period. Cycle length and bleeding patterns can vary between people and from month to month. Tracking dates, bleeding, pain, and other symptoms can help a person discuss patterns with a healthcare professional.

Ovulation timing is not identical for everyone, and calendar estimates are not guaranteed contraception or pregnancy prevention. A period-tracking prediction should be treated as an estimate rather than a diagnosis.
